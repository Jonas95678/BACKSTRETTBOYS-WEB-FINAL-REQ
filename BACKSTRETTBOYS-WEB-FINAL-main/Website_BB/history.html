Backstreet Boys About Top Hits Band's History

1993

Formed in Orlando, Florida by Lou Pearlman. Original members: AJ McLean, Howie Dorough, Nick Carter, Kevin Richardson, and Brian Littrell. First major performance at SeaWorld to 3,000 fans.

1996

International debut album released. Won MTV Select Award for "Get Down (You're The One For Me)" at the MTV European Music Awards.

1997

Released second album "Backstreet's Back." Won MTV Select Award for "As Long As You Love Me."

1998

Won Billboard Award for Group Album of the Year, MuchMusic Video Award for Favorite International Group, MTV VMA for Best Group Video ("Everybody"), and World Music Award for World's Best-Selling Dance Artist.

1999

Swept awards including Teen Choice, Kids' Choice, and American Music Awards. Dominated Billboard Music Awards with Artist of the Year, Album of the Year, and multiple other categories.

2000

Released fourth album "Black & Blue," selling 1.6 million copies in the US first week. Later certified platinum.

2006

Kevin Richardson announces his departure from the group.

2011

NKOTBSB supergroup formed with New Kids on the Block. Album debuted at #7 on the US charts.

2012

Kevin Richardson returns. Performed on Good Morning America's Summer Concert Series to the largest crowd in GMA history.

2013

Celebrated 20th Anniversary. Released "In A World Like This," debuting at #5 on Billboard 200—making them the first act since Sade with nine US top 10 albums.

2014

Won MTV Movie Award for Best Musical Moment ("This Is The End"). Inducted into Walk of Fame in Locarno, Switzerland.

2015

Brian Littrell and Kevin Richardson inducted into the Kentucky Music Hall of Fame.

2018

Released "Don't Go Breaking My Heart," their first single in five years.

2022

"A Very Backstreet Christmas" released—their tenth album and first Christmas album. Debuted at #1 on Billboard Top Holiday Albums and #17 on Billboard 200.

2024

Celebrated 30th anniversary with fans in Cancun, Mexico.

2025

Millennium 2.0 announced on Today Show, releasing July 11th to celebrate the 25th anniversary of their 5-time GRAMMY-nominated chart-topping album.
